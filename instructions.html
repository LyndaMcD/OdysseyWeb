So, you found it.

If you've made it this far, you already know that this isn't about one story, one book, or even one trickster.

Tricksters are everywhere.

Every culture has its own versions: fools, thieves, shapeshifters, rule-breakers and troublemakers. Their names and stories change from place to place, but somehow, they keep appearing.

And there's one here too.

Somewhere in this library are markers left behind to guide you. Follow them. Pay attention to the books, objects and traces you find along the way. They'll help you uncover the trickster connected to this place.

When you think you know who — or what — you've found, create a Trickster Case File.

Record what you discovered, where you found it, and what makes you think you've identified the right trickster. Your file will become part of the archive alongside the discoveries made by other players.
